# Subject Package — Steelpan

Everything the production engine needs to make a steelpan-history video that is
on-brand, legally safe, and aimed at the right audience.

Read by: openmontage's `skills/meta/creative-intake` and `research-director`
(via `montage_ext/`), and by the hook & script scoring loop.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `profile.yaml` | Brand, audience, platform targets, content pillars, cadence. |
| `copyright-rules.md` | **Hard rules.** What may and may not appear on screen. Enforced, not advisory. |
| `fact-corpus/` | Vetted facts the research stage must cite *before* going to the open web. Sourced from the Series 1 doc and *Forged in Carnival*. |

## Series 1 — "Pan: The Story"

Scope: history, pioneers, note layout, the music. **No product content, no
Chrome Factory process footage, no selling.** It is the only content block that
can be produced before any filming happens, because it needs none of our own
footage — it runs on motion graphics, animated diagrams, text-on-screen,
public-domain stills, and licensed audio.
