# Steelpan Subject Package — copyright & authenticity rules

**These are hard rules. The production engine must enforce them, not treat them
as suggestions.** Every asset that reaches the timeline is checked against this
list. A violation blocks the render (Gate 2), it does not warn-and-continue.

History content is the hardest short-form category to source legally. Name the
constraint before scripting, not after.

---

## MUST NOT appear on screen

| Not allowed | Why |
|---|---|
| Archival photographs of Spree Simon, Ellie Mannette, Anthony Williams, Neville Jules, Bertie Marshall, Rudolph Charles, TASPO members, or any named historical figure | Copyright, ownership often unclear/unclaimable |
| Panorama, steelband, or Carnival performance footage or photographs | Copyright held by bands, broadcasters, organisers |
| Commercial pan recordings or released steelband music | Licensed music; use only platform-cleared audio libraries |
| Documentary clips, news footage, broadcast material | Copyright |
| **AI-generated depictions of real historical figures or of specific historical pans** | Prohibited under Pipeline v1.0 §2.3 — authenticity violation, cannot be waived by a disclosure caption |
| Any asset without recorded provenance (source + URL + licence) | Cannot prove the right to use it |

## MAY appear on screen

| Allowed | Notes |
|---|---|
| Original diagrams and animation | Note layouts, timelines, maps, cycle of fourths/fifths, pan-family charts. The workhorse of this series. |
| A steelpan we own, filmed by us | Deferred — no footage yet. When available, it carries most of the visual load. |
| Our own recordings of a pan being played | Us, or a player who has given written permission |
| Public-domain / CC0 stills | Ken Burns. Provenance still required. Verify the licence, do not assume. |
| Text-on-screen with sound design | Viable for 20–30s facts if the typography is strong |
| Platform-licensed audio | Only tracks the platform's own library clears for the target platforms |
| Generic, non-figurative AI imagery | Textures, abstract motion, an oil drum as an object — never a named person or a claimed-historical pan |

## Attribution

Where a CC licence requires credit, the credit goes in the video description
and, if the licence requires it on-screen, as a small persistent caption.
`montage_ext/` writes the required-attribution list into the publish bundle.

## When in doubt

Treat it as not allowed and use a diagram or motion-graphics treatment instead.
The series is designed so that "one pan + original diagrams + motion graphics +
licensed audio" can carry every episode.
