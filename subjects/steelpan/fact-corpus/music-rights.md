# Music & audio rights

Source: Series 1 doc §6.

## Can be posted
- Our own recordings of a pan being played, by us or by a player who has agreed.
- Single struck notes, scales, arpeggios, short phrases — all original recordings.
- Platform-library audio where the licence permits it for the target platform.

## Cannot be posted
Commercial pan recordings, Panorama performances, and arrangements by named
arrangers are all licensed material. Using them risks takedown, muting, or a
distribution penalty — and a muted video is a **total loss** on a platform where
the audio carries the payoff.

## The workaround
Record our own audio library. One session with one pan and one player gets:
individual notes across the range, a chromatic run, a few chords, a short
original phrase, and the pan's ambient decay. That library covers the entire
series and costs one afternoon.

Until that session happens, Series 1 audio = platform-licensed music beds only,
no pan performance audio.
