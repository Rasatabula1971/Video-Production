# The headline claim

Source: Series 1 doc §3.1.

> **"The only acoustic instrument invented in the 20th century."**

This is the claim to lead the series with. It is the strongest single hook
available and it is well supported — the United Nations uses this framing in its
description of the steel pans donated by CARICOM in 1995.

## Two cautions — both are non-negotiable

1. **"20th century," never "19th."** Getting this wrong in the opening video of a
   pan account is not recoverable.
2. **Use the qualified form.** "The only acoustic instrument invented in the 20th
   century" is defensible. Dropping "acoustic" invites correction — the theremin,
   the electric guitar and others are 20th-century instruments.

The hook & script scoring loop must flag any script that states the claim without
the word "acoustic," or that dates it to the 19th century, as a hard fail.
